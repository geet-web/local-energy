The Process

How It Works

Going solar with Local Energy is simple. We do the heavy lifting so you just sit back and save.

01

Book Consultation

Call us or fill the form. Our solar advisor will connect with you within 24 hours to understand your energy needs and answer all questions.

02

Site Assessment

Our certified engineer visits your property to assess roof area, shadow analysis, load requirements, and structural suitability — all free of charge.

03

Installation

Our trained technicians install a premium Tier-1 solar system, applying for net metering and government subsidies on your behalf simultaneously.

04

Start Saving

Your system goes live! Monitor every unit generated on our app, enjoy slashed bills, and watch your investment pay itself back month after month.
