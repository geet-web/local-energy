Cost & Investment
A residential solar system typically costs between ₹45,000 to ₹60,000 per kW before subsidies. A 3 kW system for a typical Indian home costs around ₹1.5 to ₹1.8 lakh before subsidy. After the PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000), your net investment can be as low as ₹75,000 to ₹1 lakh.
Most residential solar systems see a return on investment (ROI) in 2.5 to 4 years. After that, you generate effectively free electricity for the remaining 21+ years of your system's life. On a 3 kW system with a ₹3,000 bill, your ROI is typically under 3 years.
There are no hidden charges. The only potential ongoing cost is an optional Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC), which covers panel cleaning, inverter checkups, and emergency support. AMC plans start as low as ₹3,000 per year for a 3 kW system.
Yes. We partner with leading banks and NBFCs to offer attractive solar loans at interest rates starting from 7% per annum. Many customers find that their monthly EMI is lower than their current electricity bill — meaning they're cash-flow positive from day one.
Subsidies & Benefits
PM Surya Ghar is a central government initiative that provides direct subsidies for residential rooftop solar. You get ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000/kW for the 3rd kW — up to a maximum of ₹78,000. We handle the entire application and approval process for you.
Any Indian homeowner who owns the property (or has the owner's consent) and has a valid DISCOM electricity connection is eligible. Subsidies are for residential connections only — commercial and industrial units do not qualify for PM Surya Ghar but can avail accelerated depreciation benefits.
Once your installation is complete and submitted, subsidy processing typically takes 30–90 days. The amount is directly credited to your registered bank account via DISCOM. We track the status and keep you updated throughout.
Installation Process
A standard 3–5 kW residential installation is completed in 1–2 days. Larger commercial systems (20–100 kW) typically take 3–7 days. The entire process from booking to going live — including DISCOM approvals and subsidy filing — is completed within 7–14 working days.
Our mounting systems use hook-based or ballasted (weight-based) structures that require minimal or zero drilling on most RCC (concrete) roofs. For metal or tin shed roofs, we use clamp systems. Our certified installers ensure there is zero risk of leakage.
Your electricity supply remains uninterrupted during the entire installation. There may be a brief (1–2 hour) shutdown on the day of inverter connection for safety, but this is coordinated with you in advance and done early morning to cause minimal disruption.
For individual homes: no. For apartments and flat owners: you'll need your housing society committee's NOC. Our team assists with drafting the NOC application and has templates that most societies approve quickly. We also help negotiate with RWAs if needed.
Performance & Output
In most Indian cities, a 1 kW solar system generates approximately 4–5 units (kWh) of electricity per day on average, or 120–150 units per month. So a 3 kW system generates 360–450 units per month — enough to cover the average Indian household's consumption.
Solar panels still generate electricity on cloudy or overcast days — typically 20–40% of peak output. During monsoon months, generation drops, but remains steady enough to significantly offset bills. You remain connected to the grid and draw power as needed.
Yes. All systems we install include a Wi-Fi-enabled monitoring portal and mobile app. You can track real-time generation, energy exports, savings, and detailed performance analytics — anytime, from anywhere, completely free.
Maintenance & AMC
Solar systems are largely maintenance-free. The primary maintenance activity is panel cleaning — every 2–3 months using plain water to remove dust, which typically takes 30 minutes. Inverters should be checked annually. With our AMC, we do all of this for you.
Our AMC plans include quarterly panel washing, annual inverter health check, string performance testing, monitoring report review, and priority emergency response for any faults. Plans start from ₹3,000/year for a 3 kW system.
Warranty & Support
Solar panels come with a 25-year linear performance warranty from the manufacturer (minimum 80% output at year 25). Inverters carry a 5–10 year warranty. Our installation workmanship is guaranteed for 5 years — covering wiring, mounting, and connections.
Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email. For in-warranty issues, we dispatch a technician within 48–72 hours at no charge. Our monitoring system also automatically detects performance drops and alerts us — we often know about a fault before you do.
Grid & Net Metering
Net metering allows you to export excess solar energy back to the DISCOM grid in exchange for credits on your electricity account. When your panels produce more than you consume (typically daytime), those units are credited to you and offset future bills — or earn you a cheque from the utility.
Yes, with battery storage. An off-grid or hybrid system with lithium battery backup allows you to run completely independent of the grid. This is costlier but makes sense for areas with frequent power cuts. We offer hybrid systems combining solar generation with battery storage for 24/7 power independence.
Standard grid-tied solar systems do NOT work during a power outage (for safety reasons — to prevent back-feeding the grid while linemen work on it). If you need power during cuts, you need to add a battery backup or choose a hybrid inverter system — which we offer.
