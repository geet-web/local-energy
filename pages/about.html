Our Story

15 Years of Powering Indian Homes

Founded in 2009 in Lucknow, Local Energy started as a two-person team helping families in Uttar Pradesh reduce their electricity bills. Today, we're a 200+ member organisation with over 1,200 successful solar installations across Lucknow, Kanpur, Delhi NCR, and 5 other cities.

We are MNRE-empanelled, ISO-certified, and trusted by thousands of homeowners, factories, hospitals, and schools. But we still believe in treating every customer like they're our first — with complete transparency, honest pricing, and after-sales support that never ends.
