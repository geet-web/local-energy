Step by Step

Your Journey to Free Electricity

Every step is handled by our team — you just need to say yes, and we take care of everything else.

Step 01

Book a Free Consultation

Call us, fill our form, or WhatsApp — whichever is easiest. We'll schedule a free site visit within 24–48 hours at a time that's convenient for you. No commitment, no fees.

Free of cost No obligation Site visit within 48 hours
Step 02

Site Survey & Custom Design

Our certified engineer visits your property to measure roof dimensions, check orientation, assess shading, and analyse your last 12 months of electricity bills. Within 24 hours, you receive a full savings report and a custom system design for your specific roof.

Shadow analysis Bill review System design Savings report
Step 03

Documentation & Subsidy Filing

Once you approve the design, our team handles all the paperwork — DISCOM approvals, net metering application, PM Surya Ghar subsidy form, and structural report. You sign once; we handle the rest.

DISCOM approval Subsidy filing Net metering Structural NOC
Step 04

Professional Installation

Our MNRE-certified technicians arrive, set up the mounting structure, install Tier-1 solar panels, wire the inverter, and commission the system — typically within 1–2 days. Zero mess, zero structural drilling beyond what's needed.

MNRE certified team 1–2 day install Tier-1 panels Clean installation
Step 05

Go Live & Start Saving

Your system is live! We walk you through the monitoring app, show you your real-time generation data, and hand over all warranty documents. Your first electricity bill will already reflect the savings. We continue supporting you with AMC services for years to come.

App walkthrough Real-time monitoring Warranty docs Ongoing support
