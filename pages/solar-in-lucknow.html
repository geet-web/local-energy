Government Subsidies

Subsidies Available for Lucknow Homeowners

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Under this central scheme, residential solar buyers get ₹33,000 per kW subsidy for first 2 kW and ₹19,800 per kW for the next 1 kW — up to a maximum of ₹1,08,000 for a 3 kW system.

UP State MSME Incentive

Small businesses and factories in Lucknow Industrial Area can avail additional state-level incentives under the UP MSME Solar Policy. We handle all DPIIT and UPCIDA paperwork for you.

Accelerated Depreciation (AD Benefit)

For commercial installations, 40% accelerated depreciation on solar assets in the first year significantly reduces your effective system cost and shortens the ROI period.
