Understanding Your ROI

How Solar ROI Works

Immediate Bill Savings

From the very first month your system goes live, you stop paying for units your panels generate. This saving compounds over 25 years.

Net Metering Credits

Any energy you don't use gets credited to your DISCOM account and can offset future bills — or even earn you a check from the utility.

Government Subsidies

Central and state subsidies directly reduce your upfront cost, cutting the ROI period from 6 years to as little as 2–3 years.
